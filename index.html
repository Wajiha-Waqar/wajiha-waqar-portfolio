Project 01

Research Paper Assistant Agent

  • Research
  • AI / ML
  • Systems
Problem
Academic research means switching between several AI tools and research sites for literature review, summarization, drafting and citation organization, while every claim still needs to stay grounded in real papers.
Approach
The pipeline was classified as a workflow (prompt-chaining), not an agent. Each tool has one responsibility: NotebookLM grounds answers in uploaded PDF sources, Claude handles reasoning and drafting, ChatGPT improves formatting and readability.
Methods
An agent design document (job-to-be-done, inputs and outputs, tool access plan), a draft system prompt with academic-integrity rules, five evaluation cases, risks and guardrails, and a four-iteration build log.
Outcome
An MVP built and tested end-to-end on five research papers. Human review remains required for citation accuracy, interpretation and final approval. Zotero and GitHub storage are planned, not implemented.

Project 02

Leakage-Aware Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring

  • Research
  • AI / ML
  • Data
Problem
Content teams have more pages worth reviewing than they can inspect. The question: can March-only, non-label-derived search and engagement signals rank content that later meets a predefined refresh-review condition in April?
Approach
A ranking and triage system to support human review, not automated publishing. Month-to-month development panels (Jan→Feb, Feb→Mar) with a held-out future test window (Mar→Apr), so evaluation never sees the future.
Methods
A 26-feature set from search position, GA4 engagement and traffic-channel signals; a final leakage audit; Logistic Regression and Random Forest compared with client-grouped validation and selected on Average Precision; a tiered review queue with reason codes.
Outcome
On the held-out window (base rate 23.74%), the Random Forest reached Precision@20 of 0.65 against 0.40 for the Week-4 baseline, and Average Precision of 0.4838 against 0.3807. This supports ranking pages for review; it makes no causal claim about refreshing content.
